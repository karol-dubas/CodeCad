# Dumbbell Stand — Engineering Specification

## Purpose
Low-profile floor stand for a single adjustable dumbbell (printed in pairs). Elevates the handle just enough for the largest weight plates to clear the floor by ~10 mm, enabling quick plate swaps without lifting the full weight high.

## Mating Part Measurements (Adjustable Dumbbell)
- **Largest weight plate diameter:** 240 mm
- **Handle grip diameter:** 28 mm
- **Handle clear length (between inner collars/stops):** 127 mm
- **Maximum dumbbell weight:** up to 20 kg

## Functional & Ergonomic Requirements
- **Hand grip clearance:** Minimum 90 mm unobstructed central gap around the handle grip zone so the user can grasp and lift the bar comfortably without pinching fingers.
- **Collar clearance:** The upper saddle section must fit inside the 127 mm handle span with lateral margin so it never rubs against the inner weight plates.
- **Anti-racking stability:** Must resist lateral wobble along the handle axis when dropping a 20 kg dumbbell or sliding plates on/off. Symmetrical inner and outer base buttresses are used below the weight plate arc clearance zone.
- **Single-piece printability:** 100% flat base on the build plate (no bottom arch under the bridge) so the entire stand prints upright as one rigid piece without supports.
