# Valve Knob (Butterfly) — Engineering Specification

## Purpose
Replacement 2-wing butterfly valve knob designed for comfortable finger grip and support-free FDM 3D printing on a Bambu Lab P1S.

## Mating Part Measurements (Valve Stem & Sleeve)
- **Valve stem:** `5.0 x 5.0 mm` square shaft with ball tip, total engagement depth `10.0 mm`
- **Protruding brass thread collar:** `13.0 mm` clearance diameter, `5.0 mm` skirt depth
- **Outer chrome valve sleeve:** `18.5 mm` diameter
- **Adjacent lower knob clearance:** Maximum wing radius strictly `< 23.0 mm` (chosen `19.5 mm` -> `39.0 mm` total span)

## Functional & Printability Requirements
- **Design choice:** Butterfly 2-wing handle selected as the canonical version.
- **Print orientation:** Front face flat on `Z = 0` with `0.8 mm` 45° build-plate chamfer (no bottom fillets) and internal lead-in chamfers for easy installation onto the square stem.
